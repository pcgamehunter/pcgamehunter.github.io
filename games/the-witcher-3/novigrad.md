# Novigrad
The city-state of Novigrad, also called the Free City is the largest city in the North and home to the biggest navy. It's neutrality and harbour have made it one of the largest trading hubs on the Continent.

# Quest tips
- When doing Get Junior and The Gangs of Novigrad, going into the casino first and betting will allows you to win a lot of money. Then when you're forced to fight, simply leave one enemy alive and you can then do the quest with Cleaver. The arena you should do with Cleaver as completing it, doesn't provide any benefit. For helping Cleaver with each place he will offer 150 Crowns (300 total).
